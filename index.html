About Me

I am a computational biologist with over 9 years of research experience focused on understanding and combating antimicrobial resistance (AMR) through bioinformatics and machine learning.

I completed my Ph.D. from the University of Delhi South Campus (Awarded: February 2024) under Prof. Manish Kumar at the Bioinformatics & AI Lab. My doctoral work focused on characterization and analysis of antimicrobial resistance using in-silico tools.

Currently serving as Assistant Professor (Grade-I) at the Department of Biotechnology, Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida. Previously, I was a Project Scientist-I at the Translational Bioinformatics Group, ICGEB, New Delhi, and a Research Associate at the Cancer Science Institute, NUS Singapore.

I serve as Editorial Board Member, BMC Microbiology (2025–Present) and Steering Committee Member, InCoB 2025.

APBioNET Member ESCMID Member ASM Global Outreach BIDDS Member BIOCLUES Member

Research Focus

My work bridges computational methods with pressing challenges in infectious disease biology

🦠

Antimicrobial Resistance

Evolution, spread, and molecular mechanisms of antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) in ESKAPE pathogens. Investigating OXA, AmpC, and β-lactamases.

AMRESKAPEβ-lactamases
🤖

Machine Learning in Biology

ML-based tools for identification and characterization of AMR proteins and genes in -omics datasets. Built BacEffluxPred, β-LacFamPred.

MLSVMHMM
🔬

Structural Bioinformatics

Molecular dynamics simulations and docking to investigate structural impacts of mutations — DRD2 receptor, HIV-host protein interactions.

MD SimulationDockingCADD
🧫

Metagenomics & Surveillance

NGS-based metagenomic analysis for surveillance of ARG diversity across clinical, environmental, and animal reservoirs using BacARscan.

NGSWGSMetagenomics
💊

Drug Repurposing

Investigating antiviral compounds against Chandipura virus via high-energy interaction analysis of the RdRp domain of polymerase protein L.

AntiviralsRdRpDocking
🎗️

Cancer Biology

Mutational landscape of microsatellite unstable cancers at Cancer Science Institute, NUS Singapore. Mutational signatures at microsatellite repeats.

MSICancerMutation
